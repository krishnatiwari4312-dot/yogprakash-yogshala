A journey of dedication

"The practice of yoga is a journey of learning, patience and self-discovery."

Years of practice.
A lifelong pursuit.

For Master Omprakash Tiwari, yoga is more than a collection of physical postures. It is a discipline that brings together movement, breathing, awareness and consistency.

With 26 years of teaching experience and a Master in Yoga, his journey reflects a long-standing commitment to learning and sharing the practice of yoga.

At Yogprakash Yogshala, this experience is brought into live online classes, giving students an opportunity to practise from their own space and build a routine around their individual needs.

The aim is to make yoga a meaningful part of everyday life — approached with attention, patience and respect for the practice.
